DENALI

72,000 full time-resolved histograms of hidden-object scenes, captured on a low-cost ams TMF8828 SPAD LiDAR co-located with an Intel RealSense RGB-D camera. Spans 60 object configurations, 100 gantry positions, two lighting conditions and two sensor resolutions, with paired tracking-RGB and Mitsuba 3 digital-twin renders for every capture.
