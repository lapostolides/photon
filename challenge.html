CVPR 2027 · Community benchmark

The All-Photon Perception Challenge

A benchmark built entirely on consumer LiDAR. Two tracks — line-of-sight 3D reconstruction and non-line-of-sight perception — on hardware that costs about fifty dollars, so the barrier to entry is the algorithm and nothing else.

Go to the challenge site → Tracks, datasets, toolkit and leaderboard live there
Track 01

Line-of-sight 3D reconstruction

Recover scene geometry from sparse, low-resolution time-of-flight histograms of the kind a phone or robot actually produces.

Track 02

Non-line-of-sight perception

Detect, track and reconstruct objects outside the sensor's field of view from indirect bounces alone.

Toolkit

Contribute

A shared capture toolkit and a $50 sensor mean participants can extend the benchmark with their own data rather than only fitting to ours.
