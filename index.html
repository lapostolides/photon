Oct 2009

The principle of seeing around corners

Kirmani et al., “Looking around the corner using transient imaging.” The first formulation of hidden-scene recovery from multi-bounce light.

ICCV
Marr Prize, Hon. Mention Mar 2012

First experimental demonstration around a corner

Velten et al., “Recovering three-dimensional shape around a corner using ultrafast time-of-flight imaging.”

Nature Communications Jul 2013

Femto-photography

Velten et al., “Capturing and visualizing the propagation of light.” Light in flight, filmed at a trillion frames per second.

ACM TOG · SIGGRAPH
Test of Time Award May 2015

DARPA launches REVEAL

“Shedding light on untapped information in photons.” The field the lab opened becomes a national research program.

Program announcement Sep 2016

Imaging through thick tissue, and through closed books

Satat et al., “All photons imaging through volumetric scattering,” alongside Redo-Sanchez et al. on terahertz time-gated spectral imaging.

Scientific Reports
Nature Communications May 2018

Photography through realistic fog

Satat et al. Time-resolved measurement separates the photons that travelled straight through from those that scattered.

ICCP Jun 2023

Two-bounce imaging with a single SPAD

Somasundaram et al., “Role of transients in two-bounce non-line-of-sight imaging.” The pivot from lab lasers toward commodity sensors.

CVPR Jun 2024

PlatoNeRF

Klinghoffer et al. 3D reconstruction in Plato's cave — recovering full scene geometry from a single view and two-bounce lidar shadows.

CVPR 2025

Commodity sensors, real tasks

Handheld 3D scanning from diffuse lidar and RGB (Behari et al.), and the first robot to navigate by what it sees around a corner (Young et al.).

CVPR · ICRA Apr 2026

Seeing around corners with the LiDAR in your phone

Somasundaram et al., “Imaging hidden objects with consumer LiDAR via motion induced sampling.” Capability that once needed a $100k laboratory, running on a $50 smartphone-grade sensor.
