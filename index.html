History of Single-Photon Imaging

2008

Camera Culture founded

The group is established at the MIT Media Lab under Ramesh Raskar.

MIT Media Lab
2009

The principle of seeing around corners

Kirmani et al., “Looking around the corner using transient imaging.” The first formulation of hidden-scene recovery from multi-bounce light.

ICCV
Marr Prize, Hon. Mention 2012

First experimental demonstration around a corner

Velten et al., “Recovering three-dimensional shape around a corner using ultrafast time-of-flight imaging.”

Nature Communications 2013

Femto-photography

Velten et al., “Capturing and visualizing the propagation of light.” Light in flight, captured at an effective trillion frames per second.

ACM TOG · SIGGRAPH
Test of Time Award, 2024 2016

Imaging through scattering media, and through closed books

Satat et al., “All photons imaging through volumetric scattering,” through a 15 mm tissue phantom, alongside Redo-Sanchez et al. on terahertz time-gated spectral imaging.

Scientific Reports
Nature Communications 2018

Photography through realistic fog

Satat et al. Time-resolved measurement separates the photons that travelled straight through from those that scattered.

ICCP 2023

Two-bounce transients for hidden-scene imaging

Somasundaram et al., “Role of transients in two-bounce non-line-of-sight imaging.” Shows that time-of-flight lets shadows from multiplexed illumination be separated, measured with a scanned single-pixel SPAD.

CVPR 2024

PlatoNeRF

Klinghoffer et al. 3D reconstruction in Plato's cave — recovering full scene geometry from a single view and two-bounce lidar shadows.

CVPR 2025

Commodity sensors, real tasks

Handheld 3D scanning from diffuse lidar and RGB (Behari et al.), and autonomous navigation using hidden objects imaged with single-photon LiDAR (Young et al.).

CVPR · ICRA 2026

Seeing around corners with the LiDAR in your phone

Somasundaram et al., “Imaging hidden objects with consumer LiDAR via motion induced sampling.” Hidden-object imaging on a smartphone-grade LiDAR, with off-the-shelf hardware under $100.

Nature
cornar.media.mit.edu 2026

DENALI

Behari et al., “A Dataset Enabling Non-Line-of-Sight Spatial Reasoning with Low-Cost LiDARs.” 72,000 hidden-object scenes captured on a consumer SPAD sensor.
